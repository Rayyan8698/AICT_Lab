About Us

The Karachi Institute of Economics and Technology (KIET) is a higher-education institution in Karachi, offering programs in technology, engineering, business and the sciences, supported by modern laboratories, libraries and campus facilities.

Our mission is to provide accessible, high-quality education that develops critical thinking, professional skills and ethical leadership.
